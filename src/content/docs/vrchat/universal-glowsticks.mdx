## Description

_Universal AudioLink Glowsticks_ is a set of non-destructive glowstick prefabs and an editor tool that allow you to add glowsticks that shine light on your avatar. (almost) Any avatar is supported!

## Usage

### Requirements

- [Poiyomi Shaders](https://www.poiyomi.com/)
    - Other shaders may be compatible, but are not guaranteed to work. If you want to use a different shader, you will need to modify the included materials and reassign them to the prefabs.
- [VRCFury](https://vrcfury.com/)
- [AudioLink](https://github.com/llealloo/audiolink)

### Import and Position prefab(s)

1. Import the provided Unitypackage file into your avatar project.
2. Drag `(prefab name).prefab` onto your avatar in the scene hierarchy. (No need to unpack.)
3. Position the prefab in the desired location on your avatar.
    - You can place as many glowsticks as you need.
    - You can move, scale, and rotate each glowstick as needed.
4. Modify the VRCFury Armature Link component on each glowstick to link it to the correct bone on your avatar.
    - For example, if you want a glowstick to stick to your right hand, change the `Link To (Avatar)` dropdown to `Right Hand`.

### Bake Emission Mask

1. In the Unity editor, select `Tools > Cuebitt > Glowstick Emission Baker` from the top menu.
2. In the window that appears, drag your avatar and glowsticks into the fields.
    - Drag the GameObject holding your avatar's mesh into `Target Mesh`.
    - After specifying a target mesh, choose the target material from the dropdown. If your avatar has multiple materials, you will need to repeat this process for each material you want glowsticks to affect.
    - You can select this GameObject by finding it in your avatar's hierarchy or clicking on your avatar's model twice in the scene view.
3. For each glowstick, click the `Add Glowstick` button and drag the glowstick prefab from your hierarchy into the new field that appears.
4. Modify the `Light Configuration` and `Output` sections to your liking.
    - Hover your mouse over each field to see a tooltip with more information about what it does.
5. Click the `Bake Emission Mask` button to generate the emission mask.

### Import Emission Mask

1. Select the GameObject containing your avatar's mesh in the hierarchy.
2. In the inspector, find the material you baked the emission mask for and expand the `Special FX > Emission _` section.
    - Select the first empty Emission section.
3. Drag the generated emission mask texture from your project window into the `Emission Mask` field.
